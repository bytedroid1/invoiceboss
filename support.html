Moving to a new phone

On the old phone open Backup & export → Move to a new phone → Send from this phone. On the new phone open the same screen and choose Receive on this phone, then scan the code.

  • Both phones must be on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • The code works once and expires after five minutes. If it expires, tap to show a new one.
  • Everything on the new phone is replaced by what is on the old one.

If scanning will not work, the address is printed underneath the code as a fallback.

Backups

Backup & export → Full backup gives you a JSON file containing everything: invoices, quotations, clients, saved items, payments and your settings. Keep it somewhere safe — it is the only copy other than the one on your phone.

To put it back, use Restore from backup. Restoring replaces everything currently on the device.

I paid for Pro but it is not showing

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore a purchase, signed in with the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with. A lifetime purchase never needs buying twice.

If it still does not appear, email us with the store you bought from and the approximate date, and we will help you chase it.

Cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not by us.

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Android: Play Store → profile → Payments and subscriptions.

You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

My invoice numbers are wrong

Set your own prefix and next number in Settings. Invoices and quotations number independently.

The currency is wrong

The first time you open the app it picks a currency from your phone's region. Change the default in Settings, and change it per invoice on the invoice itself.

Can I use my own logo?

Yes — add it during setup, or later in Settings. It appears on every design. A wide logo works as well as a square one.

Reporting a problem with an invoice design

If something in a PDF looks wrong — text cut off, a field missing, the layout breaking with a long description — please send us the design name and, if you can, the PDF. That is exactly the kind of report we act on quickly.
