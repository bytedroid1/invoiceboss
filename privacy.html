Privacy Policy

This policy explains what InvoiceBoss (“the app”, “we”) does with your information. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version. Your invoices, clients and prices are stored on your own device. They are never sent to us, and we have no way to read them. There is no account, no sign-up and no tracking.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no server of its own. It does not ask you to register, and it does not send your business data anywhere.

What stays on your device

Everything you type into the app is written to a private database on the device itself:

  • Your business details — name, address, email, phone, tax number, payment terms and logo.
  • Your clients — names, addresses, email addresses and phone numbers.
  • Your invoices and estimates — line items, prices, taxes, discounts, notes, payments received and any signature you draw.
  • Your saved items and app settings.

This data is removed when you uninstall the app. Nobody but you can read it, including us.

Permissions the app asks for, and why

Photos

Only when you choose to add a business logo. The image you pick is copied into the app's own storage and used on your invoices. No other photo is read, and no image is uploaded anywhere.

Camera

Only when you use Move to a new phone and scan the transfer code. The camera is used to read that one code. No photo or video is recorded, stored or transmitted.

Local network access

Only during a phone-to-phone transfer. The sending phone serves your backup over your own Wi-Fi so the receiving phone can fetch it directly. The address is protected by a single-use code, stops working after the transfer, and expires after five minutes. The data does not pass through any server of ours or anyone else's.

Things you choose to send

Emailing and sharing invoices

When you email or share an invoice, the app hands the PDF to your phone's own mail or sharing app and stops there. We do not send the message, do not see the recipient, and keep no record of it. Whatever app you choose to send with has its own privacy policy.

Backups

A backup is a plain file that you export and keep. Where it goes — a messaging app, a cloud drive, your computer — is your choice, and it is governed by whatever service you put it in.

Purchases

InvoiceBoss Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Those stores handle the payment; we never see your card details, billing address or any part of your payment method.

To know whether your subscription is active, the app checks your purchase status with the store through a subscription management service. That check involves an anonymous identifier generated for your installation, along with basic technical information such as your device type, operating system version and country. It is not linked to your name, your email or anything you have typed into the app, and it never includes your invoices or client data.

Analytics and advertising

There are none. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no advertising identifiers and no third-party trackers. This website does not use cookies and loads nothing from anyone else's servers.

Children

InvoiceBoss is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Because your data never reaches us, most data rights are already in your hands:

  • Access and portability — export a full backup at any time from Backup & export. It is plain JSON you can read.
  • Deletion — delete individual records in the app, or uninstall it to remove everything.

We hold no copy to correct or delete on your behalf. Purchase records are held by the App Store or Google Play under their own policies.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting something, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change. We will not quietly begin collecting data under an old policy.
