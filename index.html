QUESTIONS

Before you ask

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for and no password to remember.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline except buying Pro, and downloading an extra font the first time you choose it.

Will it remind me about unpaid invoices?

Yes, if you switch reminders on: on the day an invoice is due, and again if it is three days late. Your client never gets a message from the app.

Can I write invoices in Urdu or Arabic?

Yes. Client names, items and notes in Urdu or Arabic print on the PDF joined up and right to left.

What if I change phone?

Use Move to a new phone in Backup, or restore an exported backup file. Pro is restored from your store account.

Is there a watermark?

Not on anything you send. Free invoices are clean invoices. The only mark the app ever adds is PREVIEW, on a preview of a Pro design or option you have not unlocked.

Can I bill in my own currency?

Yes, and per invoice — bill one client in dollars and another in rupees if that is how you work.

Do my clients need the app?

No. They receive an ordinary PDF, by email or any app you share with. Nothing to install and nothing to sign into.
